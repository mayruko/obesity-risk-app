Obesity Risk Assessment is a small website that estimates a person's weight category from their
lifestyle. You fill in a form with your age, height, weight, eating habits, exercise, water intake,
screen time, alcohol and how you get around. You press a button and it replies with one of six 
categories, from "Normal weight" to "Obesity (Type III)".

IMPORTANT NOTE
Behind the page is a program that learned from many example people with known categories. It spots
patterns, such as weight relative to height and eating habits, and applies them to new answers. It 
is an educational estimate, not a medical diagnosis.
